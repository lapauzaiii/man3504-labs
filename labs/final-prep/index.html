Question types and scoring

Mostly higher-level reasoning: scenarios, diagnosing problems and choosing the best action. Higher-level items carry more points than recall. Little math, and calculations are quick.

Multiple choice, true/false, select all that apply, matching and ordering.

Partial credit applies to select-all, matching and ordering. Wrong selections cost points.

Final exam prep

Strategy

  1. Read the whole scenario.
  2. Identify irrelevant data.
  3. Name the concept before reading the options.
  4. For select-all, evaluate each choice as a separate true/false statement.

Week 8: Supply Chain

Cowford Coffee's six cafes sell a steady 1,200 lb of beans per month combined. Each cafe manager orders from the roaster once a month in full-pallet quantities, marketing runs a 2-for-1 bag promotion every quarter, and each stage forecasts from the orders it receives. The roaster now sees monthly orders swing between 400 and 3,000 lb. Which actions would reduce the swings the roaster sees? Select all that apply.

Cowford Brewery is launching a canning line, and getting it right takes almost everything from this semester: a forecast, a bottleneck, inventory, lean flow, quality control, sourcing and a project schedule. Let's build it together, one decision at a time.

    How this review works

    1. Read the setup for each stop. Every number you need is there.
    2. Work it out on paper or a calculator, type your answer, and press Check. If you're stuck, open the hint before the full solution.
    3. Think it through. Each stop ends with one judgment question, the kind where correct math can still lead to a wrong decision.

    These numbers are original practice data. They are not taken from the final exam or any graded quiz, so don't memorize answers; practice the method. The final has 60 multiple-choice, multiple-answer and true/false questions, and roughly 40% involve a calculation like these.

    The chain you'll follow

    Forecast→Capacity→Inventory→Lean flow→Quality and SPC→Sourcing and improvement

    Each decision feeds the next, the same way the course did.

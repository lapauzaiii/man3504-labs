The Three Families of Forecasting Methods

Different forecasting contexts call for different methods. In this phase, you'll explore when each family is strongest.

Family Logic Best When Example
Time-Series (Week 4) Future demand will resemble past patterns Stable products with sufficient history Moving average, exponential smoothing
Causal (Regression) Demand is driven by external variables Clear drivers (time, income, advertising, weather) Linear regression with trend or factors
Qualitative Expert judgment + structured opinion New products, long horizons, high uncertainty Delphi, market survey, sales force composite

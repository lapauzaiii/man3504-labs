About MAN3504

Operations Management explores production processes, supply chains and service delivery. The Fall 2026 planner runs from Week 0 through Week 16 and Finals Week, including the Week 7 midterm and Week 15 Thanksgiving break. This directory lists lab and review resources, rather than every course requirement. Cowford Holdings is a fictional holding company with eight operating subsidiaries:

  • Medical Supply — Stable contracts, high quality, predictable demand
  • Brewery — Seasonal demand, production smoothing, ingredient sourcing
  • Green Build — New market, 3-year ramp, capacity under uncertainty
  • Fitness — Capacity distribution, demand shifting by season/program
  • Logistics — Scheduling, throughput constraints, on-time performance
  • Bakery — Perishability, production flow and quality control
  • Coffee — Forecasting, service flow and responsible sourcing
  • Tech Services — Service capacity, customer outcomes and continuous improvement

Classroom cases use independent data from the graded Cowford applications. Assessment instructions and exam preparation for later weeks are available through Canvas.

How to Use These Labs

These interactive labs are practice tools to help you master course concepts. They are not graded but are designed to prepare you for formal Canvas assessments (W#.C Concept Check and W#.D Application Quiz).

Four-Phase Learning Arc

  • Phase 1: Live Session — Manipulate real Cowford scenarios and see how changes ripple through operations.
  • Phase 2: Reinforcement Lab — Test your understanding with embedded quizzes and get immediate feedback.
  • Phase 3: Guided Diagnostic — Follow a step-by-step framework to solve problems identical in structure to W#.D.
  • Phase 4: Assignment Readiness — Use the checklist to confirm you're ready for formal assessments.

Canvas Integration

Labs can be embedded directly in Canvas pages using an iframe. Your instructor will provide embedded links in each week's module.

About MAN3504

Operations Management is a 14-week course exploring how organizations design and manage production processes, supply chains, and service delivery. All course content is anchored in Cowford Holdings, a fictional holding company with five operating subsidiaries:

  • Medical Supply — Stable contracts, high quality, predictable demand
  • Brewery — Seasonal demand, production smoothing, ingredient sourcing
  • Green Build — New market, 3-year ramp, capacity under uncertainty
  • Fitness — Capacity distribution, demand shifting by season/program
  • Logistics — Scheduling, throughput constraints, on-time performance

Each interactive lab uses these anchors in the Phase 1 discovery section. The Phase 2 quizzes align directly with your W#.C Concept Check. The Phase 3 diagnostic follows the problem-solving logic you'll use in W#.D. Phase 4 is your readiness checkpoint.
